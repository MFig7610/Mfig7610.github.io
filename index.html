About the Author

Miguel Figueroa Manners is a high school student in Nixa Missouri. He became passionate about writing at the age of 12 and pursued this passion ever since. His dream is not only to be an author, but also a director to show his stories around the world. Miguel really likes the arts and loves to be part of the creative world.

Free From the Living Dead End

Free From the Living starts at the beginning of the zombie apocalypse. The story is a journal written by an optimistic high schooler named Miguel. He always fantasized of what he would do in a zombie apocalypse. He thought the apocalypse would be fun and easy. You don't do chores, you don't go to school, you're free... at least that's what Miguel thinks, but the apocalypse doesn't choose favorites. Everyone gets the same treatment. In the beginning Miguel's confident, but how long will that last?
